What the counts and intervals mean

A value such as 8/20 means eight of twenty measured passages crossed the detector cutoff. The plot uses the same measured samples as the table, excluding the separate length sweep.

Development uses seed 0. Seed 1 controls set an exploratory cutoff of at least 4, raised above their highest score when needed. Seed 2 is held out. These small calibration samples cannot establish a rare false-positive guarantee.

Controls are ordinary AI-generated passages. Their trigger rate is not a human false-positive rate. Fixed-vocabulary scoring counts distinct token IDs; the previous-token method counts distinct token pairs. Normal-test calibration is approximate for both.

Displayed 95% Wilson intervals are descriptive and do not account for correlation among repeated topics. Choosing a method using this dashboard requires a new independent confirmation set.
